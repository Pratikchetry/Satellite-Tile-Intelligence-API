GalaxEye - Backend Engineer, ML Systems - Take Home Assignment Dataset (subset of EuroSAT).

candidate_tiles/  - labelled tiles in class-named folders. Use these however you like.
eval_set/         - unlabelled tiles to classify; true labels are in eval_labels.csv.
eval_labels.csv   - ground truth for the eval_set, for measuring your classifier.

Classes: Forest, River, Residential, Industrial, AnnualCrop, SeaLake, Highway

Attribution: EuroSAT, Helber et al. (2018/2019), Sentinel-2 imagery (Copernicus). Dataset licensed MIT; contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data.
